### Huakang C Cutting Production — Master Data Foundation

The confirmed cutting-module scope is Huakang C's own Customer Order Center intake and dispatch plus controlled business-schedule XLSX intake through ledger origins, engineering-provided production BOM, purchasing of fabric/accessories with material-date replies, supervisor-maintained daily plans and clerk-entered actual output. The customer-name field is labelled 客户 throughout the cutting workspaces (formerly 洋行名) and has no internal/external classification; internal versus outsourced refers only to cutting execution resources. The ledger replaces 累计交货欠产 with 累计计划差额 and keeps 未完成套数 as a separate column.

The confirmed remaining-set formula is order total sets minus completed sets for the same order, retaining the subtraction result rather than clamping it to zero or substituting task targets; P2b calculates remaining sets from the current engineering-confirmed order-set total minus net qualified completion; an absent/rebound quantity basis remains unknown. Delivery/output progress is measured in complete matching sets, with the underlying part composition supplied by BOM; the preparation interval before first supply defaults to a three-working-day recommendation and is adjustable per task, including same-day supply (zero); revisions to an already published plan require an adjustment reason. Scheduling must support a configurable subset of materials being ready, without requiring all accessories to arrive.

Current-stage required materials and feasible batch quantities are distinguished from outstanding later-stage accessories; partial cutting readiness does not establish complete-set output. Execution includes internal cutting and outsourced cutting only, not sewing. The six required workspaces are scheduling, daily reporting, material inventory, monthly closing, master data and actual production material costs. User-facing cutting time-zone wording is 北京时间 (UTC+8); the implementation continues to use the IANA identifier `Asia/Shanghai`, with no change to date calculations. Monthly closing includes both internal production/inventory/material-cost reconciliation and subcontractor processing-fee reconciliation, payable amounts and settlement documents.

The approved UI now uses the fabric-warehouse-style horizontal workspace navigation and top-right factory tag plus usage guide, while retaining cutting's own workflow and the common project design tokens; it does not inherit spray payroll or customer-receivable rules.
