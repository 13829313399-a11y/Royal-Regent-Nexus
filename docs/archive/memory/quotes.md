- Molding
- Assembly
- Painting
- Electronic
- Slush
- Sewing
- Hair
- Sales

Sales and Assembly are mandatory; Engineering and the other sections are optional. New quotes keep Engineering selected by default for compatibility, but creation explicitly allows it to be unchecked, including slush-only products. Nonparticipating Engineering is excluded from readiness, authoritative totals and output gates; selected Engineering still requires normal saved valid costing. Existing quotes retain their persisted participation. For Huakang D only (`huakang-d`), Engineering may be explicitly saved with no material, mold or carton lines and still produce a valid zero-cost calculation for output; any entered lines and enabled fees continue through normal validation and calculation. An entirely empty Engineering save is persisted with the disabled mold-allocation switch so saved/readiness/copy evidence remains explicit. When selected, Engineering still participates; other departments and factories retain their completion rules, and edit/export permissions and version sealing are unchanged.

Draft or rejected sections are editable, review states are reviewable, and approved or not-applicable sections are complete.

Internal-quote reviewers must belong to Sales (`sales-business`) and have effective review access in the quote's factory. A nonempty employee-profile department is authoritative; legacy accounts without it need an active, explicit Sales role binding. Engineering, management and wildcard administrator access alone never establish reviewer eligibility. Legacy v2/v3 candidate lists, create/clone/header assignment and both section/whole review use this boundary; assignment names come from the active account rather than client input. Direct-output v4 owners instead require an active Sales identity and effective internal_quote:sales_edit access in the quote factory, without granting or requiring review permission.

New/clone dialogs explicitly request direct-output candidates; existing-header candidates derive workflow and original creator from the persisted quote ID. Assignment validation follows the destination workflow (including clones that explicitly retain a legacy mode), so candidate query parameters cannot relax old approval rules. The candidate API retains section_review as its compatibility default for older callers. Section self-review is controlled by `internal_quote:self_review`. Sales supervisors and sales managers receive it through their fixed positions, while administrators may grant or revoke it for an individual sales-business user through the existing personal-access override workflow.

It bypasses submitter/reviewer separation only when the current user both created the quote and is its selected business owner; it never permits self-review on another creator's quote and does not change final release rules.

New internal quotes and ordinary clones default to `direct_output` (`module_version='v4'`). Fresh products, including ordinary, JustPlay and batch products, persist optional mold allocation, testing fees, freight and lifting as disabled by default; each can be enabled independently and disabling retains the entered amounts and parameters. Existing saved choices, whole-product history reuse and copied scenarios/versions retain their source settings; component-only history assembly uses the new shared-cost defaults. Blank frontend forms default these switches off, while nonempty legacy payloads with missing switches retain their historical interpretation. Reference/formula and header recalculation keep untouched initial department payloads pending; seeded switches and component metadata alone do not mark a department as filled.

Existing `v2` section-review and `v3` whole-quote-review records retain their original evidence and workflow; explicit legacy API modes remain supported. Historical quotes enter the new workflow by copying a new version, without modifying the original. A v3 quote keeps department-owned editing and authoritative section calculations but rejects section submit/review/reopen actions. Once every participating section has saved content, a valid calculation and current dependencies, one authorized Sales submitter locks all participating sections in one immutable submission manifest. Only the selected business reviewer with effective Sales review access in the quote factory may approve or reject that whole submission.

The existing self-review-only access fallback remains limited to the selected reviewer who created the quote, and v2 section self-review and legacy final-release separation remain unchanged. Approval marks every participating section approved and makes the quote eligible for controlled export; rejection marks every participating section rejected so the whole quote is unlocked while department edit permissions remain unchanged. The frontend creates and clones quotes into v4 and renders v3/v4 participating departments in one vertically continuous, always-full-width collaboration page.

Quote Overview exposes one “添加、删除参与部门” panel to Sales/Engineering participation managers, expanded immediately below the overview header and above “包装方案与版本” so its options remain near the triggering button: every optional department is shown, checked means participating and unchecked means removed after an explicit data-impact confirmation; Sales and Assembly remain fixed and cannot be removed; Engineering is optional. Import refresh retains the current department position and editor instances. The right Quote/Department Files drawer also opens from attachment preview and closes when the pointer leaves, even after clicking within it; mouse focus does not pin it open. Its attachment viewer fits the available sidebar height, with an independently scrollable report and fixed file/sheet controls; scrolling is contained within the viewer.
